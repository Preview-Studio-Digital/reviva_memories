## 👩 Prompt Oficial: Mulher

### 🇧🇷 Versão em Português
> "Inserir a MULHER na paisagem realista, com pele realista, usando VESTIDO BRANCO, segurando a câmera com apenas um braço esticado, sem mostrar a mão, sem mostrar a câmera. Sorriso discreto mostrando levemente os dentes, olhando diretamente para a câmera. Fundo desfocado. Iluminação Golden Hour, contra-luz com raios de luz. Selfie style. Estilo ultra realista. Enquadramento centralizado."

### 🇺🇸 Versão em Inglês (Midjourney / Stable Diffusion / Flux / DALL-E 3)
> "Ultra-realistic raw selfie photo of the woman naturally blended into the realistic background landscape, realistic skin texture and pores, wearing an elegant white dress. Holding the camera with only one arm extended off-screen, completely invisible hands, no camera visible, no phone visible. Subtle gentle smile slightly showing teeth, looking directly into the camera lens with warm and serene eyes. Blurred background with depth of field bokeh. Golden hour lighting, backlighting with soft sun rays and lens flare. Authentic selfie style, ultra realistic style, centered framing, 8k resolution, cinematic photorealistic masterpiece."

* **2. Montanhas Serenas:** `Realistic majestic mountain range in the soft blurred background with golden hour sunset rays and gentle mist.`
* **3. Floresta Encantada:** `Realistic lush green forest in the soft blurred background with golden sunrays filtering through tree branches.`
* **4. Campo de Girassóis:** `Realistic radiant sunflower field in the soft blurred background bathed in warm golden hour sunset light.`
* **5. Lago Cristalino:** `Realistic calm crystal-clear lake with serene nature reflections in the soft blurred background under golden light.`
* **6. Palmeiras Tropicais:** `Realistic tropical palm trees with gentle coastal breeze in the soft blurred background under warm sunset glow.`
* **7. Vale Dourado:** `Realistic vast golden valley hills stretching to the warm glowing sunset horizon in the soft blurred background.`
* **8. Descampado Verdejante:** `Realistic lush green open grassy plains in the soft blurred background under natural sunlight.`
